Sustainable burst = transfer time ≤ page-turn time, or the queue grows without bound. At ~15 MB/s and a 4 s page turn that is two to three frames.

Focus

Live view is ~1/35 of the sensor's pixels and cannot show you focus. These scores come from a real captured frame. The number means nothing alone — turn the ring and watch it peak, then back off until it falls.

Exposure

1. Mode dial to A, aperture f/8, ISO 100.
2. Aim at the page under the lighting you will actually use.
3. Press Meter. The camera solves for shutter; we correct it.
4. Dial to M and enter the values below.
A meter renders whatever it sees as middle grey, so aimed at white paper it underexposes by about two stops. The correction below is measured from where the paper actually landed in the frame, not assumed.
The mode dial cannot be set over PTP — it is the one setting that must be moved by hand. Everything else here is applied for you.
