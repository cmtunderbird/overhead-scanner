SENSOR 2 × 24.3 MP APS-C LENS 30 mm @ f/8, locked TARGET beat CZUR ET Ultra · 441 DPI RESULT 462 DPI / 41.2 MP MOVING PARTS none
drives tile width → DPI, working distance & stereo band

Sheet 1 — Plan view. Two cameras look straight down at a flat A3 area, each covering one half of the 420 mm long axis with a deliberate overlap across the spine. Both are parallel and vertical: no toe-in, so the stitching homography is a pure translation plus a fixed calibrated correction. The lens covers 331 mm on the page's 297 mm short axis — 34 mm of field is thrown away, and that waste is what buys a clean margin for deskew.

Sheet 2 — Front elevation, viewed along the spine. The two view cones cross in the overlap band directly above the gutter. That crossing is free stereo with a ~200 mm baseline — a height precision of about 27 µm, sitting exactly where page curvature is worst. It costs nothing and is held in reserve as a dewarp input if the platen is ever inconvenient.

Sheet 3 — Side elevation, viewed along the long axis. Lamps sit low and wide so their specular lobe reflects away from both lenses. The depth-of-field band at f/8 is ±11 mm about the platen surface — the platen keeps every page inside it, which is what removes the need for focus stacking and for 3D dewarping at the same time.

Sheet 4 — Capture architecture. Two identical nodes, role assigned by hostname, same SD image on both. Pairing is by a sequence counter issued from the laptop, never by timestamp — network jitter is irrelevant because nothing in the scene moves.

Three gotchas, each worth an evening. (1) gvfs-gphoto2-volume-monitor claims the camera the instant it enumerates — Pi OS Lite has no desktop, which is precisely why Lite is specified. (2) The A6000 drops its PTP session when idle; build reconnect-on-failure in from day one. (3) capturetarget defaults vary and silently decide whether files land in camera RAM or on the card — which changes all your timing. Set it explicitly.

Sheet 5 — Processing pipeline. Everything left of the split is deterministic and metric. Everything right of it is a derivative. The archival master is written before super-resolution is ever invoked, and the two are never conflated.

Design point 20–30 mm. Clears the CZUR's 441 DPI / 39.8 MP on both axes while leaving a usable stereo band across the gutter. Below 10 mm the band is too narrow to blend reliably; above 40 mm you fall behind the machine you are trying to beat.

Do not stop past f/8. The Airy disc is already 2.7 px wide. Every further stop buys depth of field you do not need behind a platen and costs resolution you bought the sensor for.

Why the long axis gets split

Splitting the 420 mm axis asks the sensor for a 297 : 220 = 1.35 aspect against its native 1.50 — a comfortable fit, 11 % of field wasted. Splitting the 297 mm axis instead asks for 420 : 158 = 2.65 against 1.50, throwing away most of the frame and landing near 340 DPI. The choice is not stylistic; it is the difference between beating the CZUR and losing to it.
